# Phase 4 Verification Tests (Images to PDF)

Please perform these manual verifications to ensure the Images to PDF feature operates reliably:

1. **Single Image Conversion**: Upload a single PNG or JPEG image with "Fit Image" (No Margin). Convert to PDF and download. Open the resulting PDF and verify it has exactly 1 page matching the image dimensions.
2. **Multiple Image Conversion**: Upload 3-5 images (mix of JPEG and PNG). Convert and verify the output PDF contains each image on its own page in upload queue order.
3. **A4 Portrait & Landscape Formatting**:
   - Convert images with **A4 Portrait** selected. Verify the PDF pages are standard vertical A4 pages with the image scaled to fit.
   - Convert images with **A4 Landscape** selected. Verify the PDF pages are horizontal A4 orientation.
4. **Margin Controls**: Test converting with **Small (20pt)** and **Standard (36pt)** margins. Verify appropriate padding around the image.
5. **WebP / Modern Format Support**: Upload WebP or AVIF images. Verify the worker converts and embeds them smoothly via canvas fallback without error.
6. **Non-Image Rejection**: Attempt to upload a PDF or text file. Verify it is rejected during drop/selection.
7. **Cancellation & Memory Cleanup**: Convert several large images, cancel during processing, and verify the worker terminates cleanly and the file queue resets to idle.
