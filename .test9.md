# Phase 5 Verification Tests (Convert Image)

Please perform these manual verifications to ensure the Convert Image feature operates reliably:

1. **JPEG to PNG Conversion**: Upload a `.jpg` photo, select **PNG**, and click "Convert to PNG". Open the downloaded file and verify it is a valid `.png` with original image dimensions.
2. **PNG to WebP Conversion**: Upload a `.png` with transparent background, select **WebP**, and convert. Open the output and verify transparency is preserved with high compression.
3. **PNG to JPEG (White Background Check)**: Upload a `.png` with transparent regions, select **JPEG**, and convert. Verify the resulting `.jpg` renders with a clean white background behind transparent areas instead of black artifacts.
4. **Batch Conversion**: Upload 4 images of mixed types (JPEG, PNG, WebP). Convert to **WebP**. Verify all 4 images download in sequence with `.webp` extensions.
5. **Non-Image Rejection**: Attempt to upload a `.pdf` file. Verify it is rejected at upload with a clear validation error.
6. **Cancellation & Cleanup**: Upload multiple large images, start conversion, and cancel. Verify worker terminates cleanly and file queue is reset.
